ROBO WAR

Combat robotics challenge focused on strategy, control and engineering.

Robot Requirements

  • Robot must follow BOTXCEL safety specifications.
  • No dangerous systems allowed.
  • Robot must pass technical inspection before match.

Match Rules

  • Teams compete in a controlled arena.
  • Winner is decided by opponent disablement or judge decision.
  • Referee decisions are final.

Safety Rules

  • No fire, explosive or harmful equipment.
  • Battery must be properly secured.
  • Emergency stop system is recommended.

ROBO TUG OF WAR

Strength based robot pulling competition.

Robot Specifications

  • Robot dimensions and weight must follow official limits.
  • Maximum battery voltage must follow event regulations.
  • Wireless or wired control is allowed according to rules.

Match Format

  • Teams pull opponent robot using the attached rope system.
  • Matches contain multiple rounds.
  • Fast victory bonuses may apply.

Prohibited Equipment

  • No fire equipment.
  • No liquid systems.
  • No sticky materials or illegal devices.

ROBO RACE

Speed and control based robotics race.

Race Rules

  • Robot must complete the track successfully.
  • Shortest completion time wins.
  • Manual interference is not allowed during race.

Safety

  • Robot should not damage the track.
  • Unsafe robots may be removed.
