THE RULEBOOK

Please read the competition guidelines carefully before registering your team. Final instructions given by the event officials will apply during the competition.

Sections A to I are supported during registration.

TEAM REQUIREMENTS

REGISTRATION GUIDELINES

  • Each team can have a maximum of 5 members.
  • Each registered member must provide their class and section during registration.
  • Team members may be from different classes and sections.
  • Teams should select the event they intend to participate in during registration.
  • Registration is free.

GENERAL RULES

APPLIES ACROSS THE COMPETITION

  • Participants must follow the instructions given by the event organisers and officials.
  • Teams must report to the designated event area when instructed.
  • Participants are responsible for their own robot and equipment.
  • Robots must be operated only in the areas designated by the organisers.
  • Unsafe behaviour or interference with another team's equipment may result in action by event officials.
  • The decision of the designated event officials will be final during competition proceedings.

EVENT-SPECIFIC RULES

BASIC GUIDELINES

ROBO RACE

Teams must navigate the designated race course using their robot. Course instructions and timing procedures will be followed as announced by officials.

ROBO TUG OF WAR

Competing robots face each other in the designated arena. Match procedure and winner determination will be controlled by the event officials.

ROBO WAR

Robots compete in the designated combat arena. Safety requirements and event-official instructions must be followed throughout the match.

The detailed technical specifications, match formats, scoring, dimensions and other event-specific requirements should be followed from the final rules released by the ROBOKRITI organising team.

PARTICIPANT CONDUCT

PLAY FAIR • BUILD RESPONSIBLY

  • Respect fellow participants, volunteers, organisers and event officials.
  • Do not intentionally damage another team's equipment outside the permitted competition format.
  • Follow safety instructions whenever working with robots or competition equipment.
  • Any situation not specifically covered on this page will be handled by the organisers and designated officials.

UNDERSTOOD THE RULES?

Build your team, choose your event and complete your registration before 31 August 2026.
