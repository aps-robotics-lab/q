RESPONSIBLY.

Every participant is expected to maintain discipline, respect other teams and follow instructions from the event officials.

06–12 Eligible Classes
05 Maximum Team Members
01

Registered Participants

Only registered participants should take part in the competition. Team information must be submitted accurately during registration.

02

Student Eligibility

The competition is open to students from Classes 6 to 12. Junior participation covers Classes 6–8 and Senior participation covers Classes 9–12.

03

Team Composition

A team can contain up to five members. Members may come from different classes and sections.

04

Correct Information

Participants should provide correct names, classes, sections and contact information while completing the registration form.

05

Follow Official Instructions

Participants must follow instructions given by event coordinators, technical officials and other authorized event personnel.

06

Safety

Robots and equipment must be operated responsibly. Any safety instruction issued by officials must be followed immediately.

07

Arena Discipline

Participants must remain within the areas designated by event organizers and must not interfere with another team's match.

08

Officials' Decisions

Decisions made by authorized event officials during a match or event will be treated as final for the competition.

01

ROBO RACE

A speed and control challenge where teams guide their robot through the designated course.

  • Complete the designated track.
  • Maintain control of the robot.
  • Follow instructions given by officials.
Detailed technical rules: To be announced
02

ROBO TUG OF WAR

A power-based challenge where competing robots attempt to out-pull their opponent.

  • Compete inside the designated arena.
  • Follow match-start and match-stop instructions.
  • Respect the opponent and competition area.
Detailed technical rules: To be announced
03

ROBO WAR

A controlled robotic combat challenge focused on engineering, driving and strategy.

  • Only registered competition robots may compete.
  • Safety requirements must be followed.
  • Officials may stop a match when necessary.
