ABOUT ROBOKRITI

WHERE STUDENTS BUILD THE FUTURE.

APS LBS ROBOKRITI 2026 is an inter-class robotics competition designed to bring students together through engineering, creativity, problem-solving and teamwork.

From speed and precision to power and strategy, every event gives teams a different challenge — and every machine tells a different story.
