FOUR ROBOTIC ARENAS

CHOOSE YOUR ARENA

Four challenges. Different strategies. One ROBOKRITI battlefield. Choose the event that matches your team's robot and compete on 2 September 2026.

SPEED • CONTROL

ROBO RACE

Put your robot's speed, control and driving precision to the test across a challenging race course.

BASIC EVENT RULES
  • Robot must remain under team control.
  • Teams must complete the designated course.
  • Course obstacles must be negotiated safely.
  • Fastest successful run gets the advantage.
POWER • STRENGTH

ROBO TUG OF WAR

A direct test of traction, motor power and control. Pull harder, hold your ground and outlast the opponent.

BASIC EVENT RULES
  • Two competing robots face each other.
  • Robots compete within the marked arena.
  • Unsafe interference is not permitted.
  • Match decisions follow the event officials.
STRATEGY • CONTROL

ROBO WAR

Design, drive and strategise. Put your robot against another team in a controlled robot combat arena.

BASIC EVENT RULES
  • Only registered competition robots may participate.
  • Matches take place inside the designated arena.
  • Unsafe or prohibited actions can lead to disqualification.
  • Officials have final authority during matches.
DEMO EVENT

ROBO SOCCER

Bring robotics and football together. Control your robot, move the ball and aim for the goal.

BASIC EVENT RULES
  • Robots compete in the designated soccer area.
  • Teams attempt to move the ball towards the goal.
  • Robot control must remain within the competition limits.
  • Detailed rules may be announced by organisers.

WHO CAN PARTICIPATE?

ROBOKRITI is an inter-class competition for students from Classes 6 to 12. Teams can include members from different classes and sections.
